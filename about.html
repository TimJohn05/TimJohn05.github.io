About

A student who likes to build things

I'm Timothy John Lopez, a computer science student at City College of Calamba. I build Android apps with Flutter on Linux. I avoid Windows 11, so I use Wine when a Windows application is needed.

I'm inspired by open source software and the people who maintain it. Whether I am shipping a phone app or fixing a Linux setup, I like problems that can be solved with a clear plan and a small set of tools. Contributing to a project, fixing a bug, or improving a setup someone else will use is the part I enjoy most.

How I work and skills

I like problems with a clear shape. I start by understanding what is actually broken, then pick the smallest tool that can fix it and write down enough that someone else can pick it up.

Most of my work happens on Linux. I care about repeatable setups, readable code and contributing back to the projects I use. Group projects taught me to explain decisions in plain language and to keep the next person in mind.
