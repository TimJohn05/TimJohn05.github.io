About

A student who likes to build things

I'm Timothy John Lopez, a computer science student at City College of Calamba. I use Linux for development and DevOps work, and I like problems that can be solved with a clear plan and a small set of tools.

I'm inspired by open source software and the people who maintain it. Contributing to a project, fixing a bug, or improving a setup that someone else will use is the part I enjoy most. I also like the discipline of automating a task once and having it run cleanly every time.

Design philosophy and skills

Good design should make the next step obvious. I start with the message, strip back what does not carry it, then build a system that survives being resized, reprinted and reused.

My day to day work covers typography, layout, brand marks and production files for print and social. I would rather solve a problem with one strong decision than with five clever ones.
